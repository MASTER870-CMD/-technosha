ZWCAD 2D Editions

Two Editions, Built for Every Drafting Team

Choose the ZWCAD edition that matches your drafting, customization and automation requirements.

ZWCAD Standard

Essential 2D CAD for professional drafting

  • 2D drafting & DWG file support
  • Layers, blocks & dimensions
  • Text, annotations & hatching
  • Layouts, printing & plotting
  • External references
Best for:Individual CAD users, small businesses & drafting professionals who primarily need 2D CAD
Most Popular

ZWCAD Professional

Advanced 2D CAD for engineering & design teams

  • Everything in Standard, plus:
  • Dynamic blocks & tool palettes
  • LISP, VBA & .NET API support
  • ARX development & automation
  • Custom CAD workflows
Best for:Engineering, manufacturing & architectural firms needing CAD automation and customization
