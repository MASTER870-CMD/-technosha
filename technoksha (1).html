Who We Are

A small, specialist team — not a five-vendor headache.

Technoksha is a Bengaluru-based engineering technology reseller and training provider, run by founder Raksha and built around one idea: engineers shouldn't have to juggle a software vendor, a hardware vendor, a trainer and a repair shop just to keep a project moving.

We sell and support the CAD, rendering and manufacturing-intelligence software our clients actually run, train their teams to use it properly, and supply the HP and Lenovo workstations specced to handle it — for aerospace & defense, oil & gas and automotive teams where tolerances aren't negotiable.

"Most of our clients come to us after outgrowing a laptop that can't handle their assemblies, or a training course that taught the menus but not the workflow. We'd rather scope it properly once."

— Raksha, Founder, Technoksha
